52 Weeks. Production Binder.

Every week has four parts. A motivation talking-head script (Shot A, 60–90 sec). A lesson guide members read. An instruction script you narrate while you work (Shots W/C/D, 3–6 min). And a shot sheet. Film the motivation and the instruction on the same day, with the same horse and the same light.

Standard setups · from your shot sheet

Four camera setups. Every shot in this binder uses one.

A

Authority Stand

Use
Every motivation script. Week intros.
Height
Chest to chin. Eye level or just below.
Frame
Tight. Chest to just above head.
Background
Barn, fence, horse out of focus. Never a blank wall.
Body
Still on the hook. Move after the point lands.
W

Work Shot

Use
All demonstrations.
Height
Tripod or fence rail, knee to hip.
Angle
Side view. You and the whole horse in frame. Show the space between you.
Run
Let it roll. Don't stop and start.
C

Close Read

Use
The tells: eye, ear, lick & chew, head drop, feet.
Distance
Close. Fill the frame.
Mount
Fence post or tripod. Never handheld.
Narration
Optional. Silence works.
D

Detail

Use
Hands, rope, knots, tack, where your feet go.
Frame
Hands and rope fill the frame. Slow it down.
Mount
Tripod over your shoulder, or a second person.
Note
Members copy what they see here. Do it slowly and do it right.
Every session · safety check (in every lesson guide)
  • Boots with a heel and closed toe. Gloves for rope work.
  • A rope halter and a 12–14 ft lead. Never wrap the rope around your hand or body.
  • Work in a fenced, level area with good footing and no loose dogs or children.
  • Stand at his shoulder, never directly in front or behind. Know your way out.
  • Someone knows you're out there. Phone on you.
  • Rate yourself 1–10 before you start. Above a 6, do the ground-only version or stop.
Filming day · rules from your shot sheet
  • Light in front of you. Overcast is ideal. No sun behind you.
  • Phone on a tripod for any horse work. Wireless mic on for every take.
  • Film the motivation (A) first, while you're fresh. Then the instruction (W/C/D).
  • Leave 2 seconds of silence before the nail line. Silence before the nail is power.
  • Film every lesson's ground-only/adapted version too. Members need to see it done.
  • Real background. Barn, fence, pasture.
