Messages, in order

Feedback form

Put these questions into a free Google Form and send the link with the feedback message. Questions 1–2 and 13–14 give you your before-and-after numbers and your testimonials.

  1. Before the four weeks, what was your number (1–10) around your horse?
  2. What is it now?
  3. What changed most for your horse?
  4. What changed most for you?
  5. Which week helped you most, and why?
  6. Which week was hardest or least clear?
  7. Were the lessons too short, about right, or too long?
  8. Did you use the ground-only or adapted version? Was it enough?
  9. Silent Sundays: what did you notice, in your horse and in yourself?
  10. How useful was Mike's weekly video feedback? (1–5)
  11. Was anything confusing, or did anything make you feel unsafe?
  12. Would you carry on to Month 2? (Yes / Maybe / No) What would make it a yes?
  13. What would you tell a friend about the Foundation Series?
  14. May Mike share your answer to question 13, with your first name? (Yes / No)
