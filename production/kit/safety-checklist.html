Before you open the gate

  • Boots with a heel and a closed toe. Gloves for rope work.
  • Rope halter and a 12–14 ft lead.
  • Never wrap the rope around your hand, your arm, or anything else. Ever.
  • A fenced area with level footing. No loose dogs. No children running around.
  • Somebody knows you're out there. Phone on you.
  • I'll stand at his shoulder. Never right in front of him. Never right behind him.
  • I know my way out.
