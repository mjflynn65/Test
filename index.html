The approach

Confidence from the ground up.

Mike Flynn Horsemanship is built on a simple belief: the bond with your horse is formed long before you ever pick up the reins. Each week you'll get short, clear video lessons and one calm exercise to practise with your horse, at home, in your own time.

Nothing is rushed. Nothing is forced. You'll always know what you're doing, why you're doing it, and how to stop safely.

  • 01

    Safety first, always

    Where to stand, how to read warning signs, and how to step away calmly. Taught before anything else.

  • 02

    Connection over correction

    Learn to read your horse's body language and respond in a way that builds trust in both directions.

  • 03

    Your body, your pace

    Seated, standing, or ground-only. Every lesson has options for limited mobility and lower confidence.

  • 04

    Small steps, every week

    About 20 minutes a few times a week. Small wins add up to a horse you feel genuinely close to.

Month 1 · Where everyone begins

The Foundation Series

Four weeks to feel safe, steady and present beside your horse, before asking either of you for anything more.

  1. Week 1

    Your Safe Space

    Setting up a calm, safe environment. Grounding your breath and body so your horse feels your calm instead of your worry.

  2. Week 2

    Reading Your Horse

    Ears, eyes, tail, posture. Learn the quiet signals of a relaxed horse, a worried horse, and one that needs space.

  3. Week 3

    Space & Kind Boundaries

    Your personal bubble and theirs. Asking your horse to give you space, gently and clearly, without force or fear.

  4. Week 4

    Walking Together

    Leading as a partnership. Stopping, starting and turning together, so every walk to the field becomes a small moment of connection.

Gentle questions, honest answers

Do I have to ride?

No. The first nine months are entirely ground-based, and the riding months each have a full ground-only alternative. Many members never ride, and they're just as close to their horses.

I'm in my 50s/60s and not very fit. Is this realistic?

Yes. This program was designed for you. Exercises are low-impact, and every lesson shows adapted options for limited strength, balance or mobility.

How much time does it take each week?

Plan on a 15–25 minute lesson and two or three short practice sessions. Lessons stay available, so you can repeat a week whenever you need to.

What if my horse is difficult or I've had a bad experience with them?

The Foundation Series starts with safety and reading your horse, exactly what you need when trust has been shaken. If you ever feel unsafe, the guidance is always to pause and get in-person help.
